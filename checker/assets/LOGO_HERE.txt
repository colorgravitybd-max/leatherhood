UPLOAD YOUR ELHOE LOGO HERE
============================

Place your brand logo file at:

    checker/assets/logo.png

(PNG, JPG, or WEBP - the filename must be exactly  logo.png  for it to be picked up.
 Transparent PNG strongly recommended so it sits cleanly on the cream background.)

Where it appears once uploaded:
  - Customer verification page (top of the hero, ~64px tall)
  - Browser favicon
  - Admin login screen

Until you upload it, the page falls back to a clean SVG "ELHOE" wordmark in serif type.

You can delete this LOGO_HERE.txt file once your logo is in place.
